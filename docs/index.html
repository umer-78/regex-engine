ReDoS Cost Comparison

Pattern (a+)+b against a string of a with no b. No match → backtracking tries every way to split the input between the two + levels.

n = 20
Backtracking: 16,777,194 steps
Thompson NFA: 592 steps

Ratio: 28,340× more work for the backtracking engine at n=20

Closed forms: backtracking = 2^(n+4) − (n+9), Thompson = 30n − 25

What It Supports

Literals, ., character classes with ranges and negation, \d \w \s and uppercase complements, * + ? and {n,m} in greedy and lazy forms, alternation, capturing and non-capturing groups, and ^ $ anchors.

Not supported: backreferences and lookaround. They are why backtracking engines exist, and adding them would give up the linear-time guarantee this project is about.
