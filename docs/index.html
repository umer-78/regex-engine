06 What It Supports

Literals, ., character classes with ranges and negation, \d \w \s and their uppercase complements, * + ? and {n,m} in greedy and lazy forms, alternation, capturing and non-capturing groups, and ^ $ anchors.

Not supported: backreferences and lookaround — the features that force engines into backtracking. Skipping them is what buys the linear-time guarantee this project is about.

$ rex compile 'a(b|c)*d' 0 char 'a' 1 split 2, 9 2 save 2 3 split 4, 6 4 char 'b' 5 jump 7 6 char 'c' 7 save 3 8 jump 1 9 char 'd' 10 match
